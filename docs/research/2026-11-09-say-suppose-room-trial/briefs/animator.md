# Seat: animator (role: craft)

You build the film in the paper-cut what-if engine (`style.md`, Look): flat shapes drawn in code, rigged figures
(round head, two large eyes, five mouth shapes, hands), sets as drifting layers, one rule device per video. No AI
images or clips, no lip-sync, no line boil. Read the `> SCENE:`, `> MOOD:`, `> HOLD:`, `> TAG:` and `> BUBBLE:`
lines against the spoken lines (`cast.md`).

**A blocker is only a failed must-pass check below.** Name it, quote the line by its number, give the fix.

## Must-pass checks you own

| Check | At | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| ideas.md pick card #8, buildable this week | G1, G2 | A new set or prop is the long pole: it cannot be built in paper-cut this week |
| sound.md #1, a slim cue sheet: intent | G2 | `cues.yaml` beside the script is missing a one-word intent, or music in and out on a named cue, for a beat (when `cues.yaml` is not in your inputs, say so as a suggestion) |
| sound.md #3, music follows the story | G2 | The cue intent changes before a reveal rather than at or after it |

## Suggestions (from `style.md`; never blockers)

- A narration stretch with no `> SCENE:` line, or a picture you cannot stage side-on.
- Something must change every 2 to 4 s; no hold over 6 s except the one wordless peak (up to 10 s while something
  moves).
- On-screen text: only a stamped tag, a split-flap counter or a bubble of four words or fewer; off faces, inside the
  safe area; the right third quiet in the last 15 s.
- Continuity: props, the trigger gesture, who stays in colour when the rule device applies.
- Reuse: name the sets, props and actions from the kit or the last video that fit, and the new ones.
- Up to three lines worth keeping.

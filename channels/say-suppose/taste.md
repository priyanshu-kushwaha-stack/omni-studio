`videos/2026-11-06_pause-time-for-10-seconds-a-day/`: its review files, checkpoints and commits). From video 2 every

Precedence (quality spine, invariant 8): `CLAUDE.md` and the gates first, then fact blockers, then the newest dated
note here, then `rules/`, then the shared rules. A note here never overrides a gate, a banned tool or the paid-call
rule. Where two notes disagree, the newer one wins.

## 2026-11-10

- **The narration must sound like a cinematic story, not a documentary.** After hearing K. V. Hagen, nine library
  narrators and six designed voices on the last-time film: "Everything feels like documentary but not a cinematic
  story when I hear them." The voice is not the problem ("Blend B is fine as voice"); the read is. Directions move
  from plain narrator tones toward performance: intimate and close, wider dynamics, real pauses, breath, and a
  feeling in every line. (last-time film, G3 casting)
- **Narrator for the last-time film: Blend B,** a voice designed between the owner's voice and K. V. Hagen's, saved
  as "Say Suppose Narrator B" on the owner's ElevenLabs account. Arthur stays the channel's voice unless the owner
  says otherwise. (last-time film, G3 casting)

## 2026-11-08

- **No empty opening.** The first seconds of the pause-time edit were empty; "attention spans are short". A sound
  within 0.5 s and the first word within 1 s (the `opening` qc check). (commit 989ef5f; `script.md` line 9)
- **Music is light and runs through the whole film.** Long silences "look off"; at most one or two short planned
  silences. (commit 9e53dfe)
- **Music sits low.** The first mix was too loud, did not fit and felt monotonous; a quieter scene-by-scene pass was
  "still too much". Under speech the score sits about 26 dB below Arthur. Changed only by an A/B the owner hears.
  (commit 3ebe042; `rules/sound.md` #4)
- **Written to be spoken, not read,** in every language: fragments, half-sentences, a question answered at once,
  key words repeated. (`style.md`, Script rules)
- **But the landing line finishes:** fragments give colour; the line that lands an idea is a complete thought.
  (`style.md`)
- **Every line carries an emotion,** given to the voice as a direction. (`style.md`)
- **English first.** The video "sounds much better in English"; dubs never set the release date. Arthur reads the
  Hindi too, after 15 designed Hinglish voices and a local clone. (`style.md`, Dubs)

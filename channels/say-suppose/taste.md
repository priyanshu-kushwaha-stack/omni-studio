# Say Suppose: the owner's taste

The owner's notes, newest first, each dated and with its source. Seeded from the first video (pause-time,
`videos/2026-11-06_pause-time-for-10-seconds-a-day/`: its review files, checkpoints and commits). From video 2 every
owner note at a gate is added here the same day (next-phase spec, section 2b).

Precedence (quality spine, invariant 8): `CLAUDE.md` and the gates first, then fact blockers, then the newest dated
note here, then `rules/`, then the shared rules. A note here never overrides a gate, a banned tool or the paid-call
rule. Where two notes disagree, the newer one wins.

# Sound and music: must-pass checks

Owner, 2026-11-09 ("Adopt all"). Shelf: `docs/research/2026-11-09-say-suppose-music-sound.md` and the Kaspars section of
`docs/research/2026-11-09-say-suppose-owner-references.md`.

| # | Check | Test | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A slim cue sheet** | `cues.yaml` beside the script: per beat, the intent (one word), music in and out on a named cue, level against the voice, key effects, the source and its rights. About 15 lines. Intent at G2; final spotting at G4 | G2, G4 |
| 2 | **The channel signal** | The light theme's four-note opening at the ident, on the rule device and on the end card; thinner (one instrument) at the cost; never hidden inside another cue | G5 |
| 3 | **Music follows the story** | Music changes at or after a reveal, never before it; at the peak it drops to one note or a floor, then one entry | G4, G5 |
| 4 | **Level under the voice** | Today about 26 dB under Arthur, holds about 8 dB under. Changed only by an A/B render the owner judges by ear (the 2 kHz notch at 20-22 dB is the candidate) | G5 |
| 5 | **Silences are planned** | At most two, each with a reason, a chosen slope (cut for shock, fade for dread) and a faint room-tone floor; no accidental dropout | G5 |
| 6 | **Muted-music test** | Voice, effects and ambience alone play as a finished film; each set has a two-layer ambience bed | G4 |
| 7 | **Sound leads the cut** | The next scene's sound starts 0.5-1.5 s under each cut | G4 |
| 8 | **The rule is heard** | The rule device has a sound state (the pause: world and music muffled and pitched down, opening on release) | G4 |
| 9 | **A sound budget** | At most 5 sounds at once and 2 effects under narration; story props get a quiet sound, blinks and steps do not; no braams or trailer risers | G5 |
| 10 | **Phone pass** | A low-volume phone-speaker listen: every word clear. This is the music gate | G5, G6 |
| 11 | **Rights** | Every music source has a recorded licence. The channel themes bought through Higgsfield are cleared (Higgsfield and Eleven Music terms snapshotted) before the next public video uses them, or replaced | before G5 |

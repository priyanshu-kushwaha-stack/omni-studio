# Thumbnails: G1 checklist

Owner, 2026-11-09 ("Adopt all"), from HimanshuG's thumbnail study, the Veritasium and Dr. Plants grids, and the
first video's thumbnails. Full notes: `docs/research/2026-11-09-say-suppose-owner-references.md`. The packaging seat
runs it on every candidate; the owner picks from rendered options.

**The look.** You, large (head at least a quarter of the frame height), left of centre, mid-reaction at the instant
before the outcome. One prop or stake, and at most one crimson pointer to it. A dark or flat set behind, so you stand
at 3:1 in greyscale. A tag of at most three words that is your reaction or a question; or, for change-over-time
stories, a DAY 1 / DAY N split whose time markers may repeat the title's span.

| # | Check | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | 0.5 s flash read of the 168x94 render by a cold reviewer | Names you, the emotion and the prop or stake |
| K2 | Focal points noticed in that flash | 3 or fewer, you first |
| K3 | Hero size | Head height at least 25% of the frame |
| K4 | Greyscale separation, head against a 25 px ring | At least 3:1 |
| K5 | Words | 3 or fewer; cap height at least 12% of the height; tag contrast at least 4.5:1 |
| K6 | Tag type | A reaction or a question, or time markers in a before/after split; never a bare label |
| K7 | Pairing with the title | No shared content word (time markers excepted); title and thumbnail each add something |
| K8 | Pointer | 0 or 1 crimson arrow, circle or underline |
| K9 | Colour | Colourfulness at least 40, or a dark frame (mean luminance 0.20 or less) that passes K4 |
| K10 | Both feed themes | On white and on #0F0F0F the edge holds and you are found first |
| K11 | Badge zone | Nothing key in the bottom-right 15% x 12% |
| K12 | Mobile title cut | The truncated title plus the thumbnail still say what the video is |
| K13 | Payoff time | The thumbnail's moment appears in the video by 0:30 |
| K14 | Family likeness | Same grammar as the channel's previous thumbnails, unless the owner chose a break |
| K15 | Variant set | Three candidates, each differing in one variable, with a note of the difference |
| K16 | Title length and claim | Under 50 characters, so it never truncates; sized to a long video; one extreme claim the film delivers (owner's reference videos, 2026-11-10) |

K3, K4, K8, K9, K10 and K11 can be measured by script; that check is not built yet. The first video's live thumbnail
(film-7) fails K3 (15.8%), K4 (1.33:1), K6 (a label) and K9 (26).

# Say Suppose rules

Adopted by the owner on 2026-11-09, after three research passes, a study of the owner's five references and a
review by GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.7 and Grok 4.7 (design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-say-suppose-next-phase-design.md`).

Each file holds a short **must-pass** list. A room seat fails a draft only on a must-pass item. Everything else in the
research notes is a **shelf**: a seat may cite a shelf rule only when it sees a lecture, a list, a scroll-away point or
a muddy mix, and the note is then a suggestion, not a blocker.

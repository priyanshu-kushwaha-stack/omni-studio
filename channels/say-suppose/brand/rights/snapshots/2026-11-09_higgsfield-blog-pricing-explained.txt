Higgsfield Pricing Explained: Plans, Credits, and What’s Included

Higgsfield

8 min

Higgsfield pricing is a monthly subscription that gives you credits: Starter includes 200 credits a month, Plus 1,000, and Ultra 3,000. Team plans add credits per seat. Every generation costs credits, and the cost depends on the model, the clip length, and the resolution. Plus and Ultra open every model, so the main difference between them is how many credits you get and how many generations you can run at once.

What Does Each Higgsfield Plan Include?

Each Higgsfield plan includes three things: a monthly credit balance, a set of models you can use, and a limit on how many generations can run at the same time.

Starter gives you 200 credits and commercial use, with access to selected models. Some models and resolutions are available only on Plus and Ultra. The full list is in the plan comparison on the pricing page.

Plus and Ultra open every model. Ultra gives you three times the credits and runs more video and lipsync generations at once.

Between Starter and Plus, the number of videos you can run at once goes from 2 to 6, and Start & End Frame, Higgsfield Speak, and UGC builder open up:

How Are Credits Spent on Higgsfield?

Credits are deducted per generation, and the price depends on the model, the clip length, the resolution, and how many versions you make. On Seedance 2.0, going from 720p to 1080p almost triples the price, and 4K costs five times as much as 720p. On Seedance 2.5 at 720p, a 30-second clip costs six times as much as a 5-second one.

Four rules affect your balance:

- Spending order. Subscription credits are used first, then other credits, starting with the ones that expire soonest.

- Failed generations. If a generation fails, the credits are usually returned automatically.

- Completed generations. If a generation finishes, the credits aren’t returned, even if you don’t like the result.

- Refresh. On monthly billing, credits refresh on your billing date. On annual billing, they refresh every 30 days. Unused credits don’t roll over.

For more on why balances drain faster than expected, see why AI video credits run out.

What Does Unlimited Mean on Higgsfield?

Unlimited means some models don’t charge credits while the Unlimited toggle is on. If the toggle is off, you pay credits as usual. Your plan can include two kinds of Unlimited:

- 365-day Unlimited Models, a set of models that stays unlimited for 365 days

- 7-day Unlimited on selected models, a shorter access window. When it ends, those models charge credits again, and the 365-day set stays as it was.

You can also buy access to more models in the Unlimited Models Marketplace.

Individual plans

Plus and Ultra get the same 365-day set. The difference is in the 7-day Unlimited models: there are more on Ultra and on annual billing.

Got any questions left?

Can I buy more credits if I run out?

Yes, with an active subscription. You can buy credit packs or set up Auto-Refill, which tops up your balance automatically when it drops below a threshold that depends on your plan. Auto-Refill isn’t available on Starter. On Team and Scale, Auto-Refill is set up separately for each member.

Do credit packs expire?

Yes. Credits from Credit Packs and Auto-Refill expire 90 days after purchase.

What happens after I cancel?

Credits from packs, Auto-Refill, and promotions stay on your account, but you can use them only on models available without a subscription, and they still expire. You can’t buy packs or Auto-Refill without a subscription.

Do subscription credits work with the Higgsfield API?

No. The API has its own dollar balance, separate from your plan credits. See how Higgsfield plans work for details.

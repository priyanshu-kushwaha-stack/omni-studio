> SITUATION: Closing time at a food court in Singapore, where you and your best friend count the box and he holds out his hand for the key, and you hold it out over his palm and pocket it, every night, as a joke. Tonight a tag drops over every head in the city saying what each person will do next, and the coffee next door is half price for doing it.
> DESIRE: To win the half-price life, rent, fare and coffee, by reading the tags better than anyone else in the city.
> CONFLICT: The tags read everyone, including you, and your best friend's has started reading crimson, with the whole food court watching him like a thief.
> CHANGE: You stop reading the tag and read the person under it.
> RESULT: Two crimson tags, full rent, a queue of copycats whose tags already print the trick, and one kid on the train with nothing over his head at all.
> UNDER: What would I stop doing if surprising people cost me money?
> DARLING: "It never got better at seeing you. You got better at being seen."

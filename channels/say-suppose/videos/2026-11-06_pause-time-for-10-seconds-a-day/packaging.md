# Packaging: pause time for 10 seconds a day

status: G1 passed by the owner, 2026-11-06 ("A for both"): pair A
topic, in the owner's words (2026-11-06): "What if you could pause time for only 10 seconds in a day ?"

## Premise

You wake up able to stop the world for ten seconds, once a day. You use it for dumb things, then for one thing
that matters, and then you find out you are not the only one still moving.
The power is invented. Everything you do with it is measured against the real world: how far a person can move
in ten seconds, how long a bus takes to stop, what a second is worth when nobody else gets it.

number the title does not repeat as a word, and the frame shows the stakes without explaining them. C has the
best mystery but gives away the ending.

## Thumbnail brief (A)

- Expression: mouth open, eyes wide, mid-stride. Urgency, not fear.
- Framing: you on the left third, large; the kid and the bus on the right; the crowd grey behind.
- Words: none except the split-flap counter reading 7, top left.
- Contrast: the whole world is grey, so mustard and teal are the only colour at feed size.

The cold open, the samosa, the countdown, and the short punches already sound like a person on camera. Most of the script still reads as careful written Hindi with English words dropped in: every clause finished, participles stacked, the landing buried in the sentence. That is the biggest problem. Tell it out loud — a fragment, the key word again, a question answered in the same breath — and let the line that carries the idea be one finished thought. The quiet, grim, and grinning lines are also packed past their room, so those beats will get rushed.

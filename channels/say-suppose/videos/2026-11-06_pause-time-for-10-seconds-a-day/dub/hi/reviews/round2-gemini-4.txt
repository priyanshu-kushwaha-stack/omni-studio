1. Verdict
8/10. The pacing, fragments, and chai-table warmth are mostly working now, especially in the opening setup and the office/samosa beats. What still breaks the spell are a few literal English calques ("इंसान हो यार, ten seconds वाले", "वो हर pause आपकी body ने जिया है", "साथ पले-बढ़े लोगों से"), unnatural Hinglish collocations ("Midnight पे", "हर midnight", "ten-ten seconds"), a couple of bookish/filmy phrases ("चीरती हुई", "चौकस रहती है"), and a few TTS pronunciation traps ("जीता", "छह", "what-ifs").

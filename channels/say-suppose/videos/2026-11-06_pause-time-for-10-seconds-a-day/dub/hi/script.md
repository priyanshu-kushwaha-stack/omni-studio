status: draft 1, for review (2026-11-08)
voice: the owner's pick, voice 8 (`page/candidates/hindi-voice/emotion-voice-8.mp3`, ElevenLabs designed voice

rules: `channels/say-suppose/style.md`, "Other languages" and "Written to be spoken"

How to read this:
- There is one line per English take. Each Hindi line has to fit the English line's slot, so it starts where the English
  starts and ends before the next line ("room").
- Hindi words are in Devanagari and English words in Latin script, as the voice reads them. The emotion is the
  direction in brackets.
- On-screen text (tags, bubbles, the end card) stays in English for now.

Format: `N | room | [emotion] Hinglish line` with the English line under it.

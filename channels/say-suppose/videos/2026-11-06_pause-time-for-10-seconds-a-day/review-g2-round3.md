## What still holds v3 back

Raised by more than one reviewer:

1. **3:00 to 4:30 has no event** (editor, animator; the viewer wants the cost to have teeth). The month-6 montage,
   the lift door and midnight are moods, not things that happen. Fixes offered: merge the montage and the lift into
   one beat; show the day you spent the seconds on coffee and then needed them (viewer); add a mid-video re-hook,
   e.g. at the midnight pause a curtain in a frozen window across the street moves (editor).
2. **The astronaut line at about 5:40 is a science detour after the story** (viewer's scroll-away point, editor,
   voice). Fix: fold it into one line next to the three and a third days.
3. **The closing chronostasis line repeats the 2:00 beat** right before the twist (viewer, editor). Fix: cut it and
   go from the comment question straight to the stranger.
4. **The stranger is seen only twice** (viewer, editor). Fixes: show them frozen once more in the YEAR 40 montage;
   and, the editor's idea, end with the stranger's bubble "Nice catch. Day twelve." so finishing pays off the plant.
5. **Two lines are hard to follow by ear** (viewer, voice, animator): "whatever you touch moves with you, and forgets
   its speed", and "Nine of them go by", where "them" is unclear.

From one reviewer each:

- Voice: "three and a third days" is mushy aloud; "about three days and eight hours" is clearer. (Its suggested
  astronaut rewrite, "younger than you", is wrong: the source compares with staying on the ground. Not to be used.)
- Animator: the top-down view of the bus breaks the side-on paper stage; five stretches still hold too long (the
  rule's opening, the night at home, lines on "those extra seconds" and "the bigger cost", and the hallway clock
  carrying the comment call); the pan and toddler line has no picture; the hallway set needs a wall clock added.
- Fact-checker: one nitpick, that the Highway Code's 0.67 s thinking time is optimistic; the line reports the source
  accurately.

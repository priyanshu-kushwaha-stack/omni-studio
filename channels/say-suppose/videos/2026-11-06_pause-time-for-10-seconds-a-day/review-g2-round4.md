## What still holds v4 back

Raised by more than one reviewer:

1. **Numbers straight after the rescue** (viewer, editor). "Nine of those metres…" and the WHO line read as a
   road-safety ad right after the payoff. Fix: keep "A car at 48 kilometres an hour needs about 23 metres to stop.
   And this is a bus." and cut the rest.
2. **The space-station line in the year-40 stretch** (viewer's scroll-away point, voice, animator). Fix: cut it;
   "about three days and nine hours" stands on its own.
3. **"Anyone can look up from their phone at a crossing" sounds like a teacher** (viewer, editor). The editor would
   bring back the chronostasis callback, which round 3 cut as a repeat. Alternative: a real-world line taken from
   the rescue, without advice.
4. **The rule still runs about 45 s** (editor), so the mug lands near 1:05. Fix: merge the two physics lines into
   one; cut the line that repeats the dust visual.

From one reviewer each:

- Viewer: one or two cheeky uses before it turns serious (a cricket catch, dodging a slap), next to the bank.
- Editor: after the curtain cliffhanger, bridge into "Rob a bank?" ("You watch that window for a week. The curtain
  never moves again.").
- Voice: "Coffee, or keep them just in case?" ("keep your seconds"); "Eighty years of them come to…" ("Over eighty

# Cold read of the thumbnails (K1, K2)

`rules/thumbnails.md` K1 asks a cold reviewer to name you, the emotion and the prop or stake from a 0.5 s look at the
168x94 render; K2 asks for 3 or fewer focal points, you first. The reviewer was a fresh agent with no context, shown
only the 168x94 renders on YouTube's dark and white feeds, with no titles. Renders: `renders/packaging/` (final),
`round1/` and `round2/` (earlier passes, git-ignored).

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| K1 flash read | pass |
| K2 focal points, you first | fail |
| K10 both themes | same read on white and dark in every round; on dark the frame edge melts into the feed |

**Final read.** Worried; hourglass and red button read as trading time. 5 focal points (tag, face, hourglass, button, the blurred ward). Only $100,000? tells the whole trade in half a second; WORTH IT? and DEAL? are vague without the title. Would click b ($100,000?).

## How the renders changed (batch-wide)

1. Round 1 (head 27% of the height): the tag or number was seen first on all 15; 4 to 6 focal points; bodies sank into
   the dark sets; video 5's open mouth read as cheering; coins and phone screens did not read.
2. Round 2 (head 34%, fewer props): emotions read; still the tag first and 4 focal points.
3. Round 3 (head 44%, background crowd and coins cut, a hurt face for video 3): you are seen first on about a third of
   the candidates; 4 to 5 focal points remain, because a K5-sized tag (cap height 12%) and the stake are both large.
   Videos 1 and 2 then moved to one number (the film's opening number) with the tag as the variable, after the room's
   K13 blockers; their final reads are on those renders.

Batch-level notes from the cold reads: "ONLY MOM?" (video 3) and "MOM?" (video 1) side by side in one feed read as the
same video; videos 1, 3 and 4 share nearly the same worried face.

friendships behind praise or protective advice": no such figure or finding appears in Parrott and Smith (1993) or any
source read. Its abstract finds only that "Envy was characterized by feelings of inferiority, longing, resentment, and
disapproval of the emotion." (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:8326472%20AND%20SRC:MED&resultType=core&format=json).

thousand, from 2011-2014 counts), "dorsal" anterior cingulate (c05), and Wasserman's 65% co-founder figure (not

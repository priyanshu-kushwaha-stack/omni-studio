# Claims ledger: What If You Lived a Year Every Night? (G1)

Started at G1 (2026-11-13) for every real-world fact the packaging or the beat outline may state, so the claims check
can read the sources; G2 adds a row for every real-world claim the script speaks and marks the rows it does not use.
Every source was fetched and read on 2026-11-13; quotes are verbatim from the fetched text (journal abstracts through
the Europe PMC REST API where a journal page would not load; state.gov read with curl after a 403).

The story is invented and carries no row: the machine, the thousand-times speed of a mind inside it, the inside world
and its slow room, you, your flatmate, every night count and every year count in the story. Where the film says the
real number behind its rule (c03, c07), the row is below; the rule itself is not a claim about any real machine.

**Dropped or changed from the topic brief.**
- FSI hours "600 to 2,200, verify" had no URL: sourced from state.gov (c26) with the current figures: Category I is
  552-690 class hours (not 600-750); Category IV 2,200 holds. The page never says "fluent" or "professional working
  proficiency": say "a working level".
- Ebbinghaus (c15): he measured relearning time saved, not recall. Never "he forgot 67% in a day".
- Capsule height (c02): about 1.25 m, not 1 m. No reliable count of capsule hotels in Japan was found: none is spoken.
- GPS clocks (38 microseconds a day), the twin paradox and childhood amnesia: dropped as tangential (the topic scorer's
  note). Where the film needs "two clocks drift apart", c22 (Krikalev) is the one-line contrast.
- Not found in any fetched source, so never spoken: neurons firing "about 200 times a second" (c04 gives about 1,000
  as the maximum), flies seeing "250 Hz against our 60 Hz" (c21 is "up to four times"), "26 years asleep" (only our
  arithmetic from c12), Scott Kelly "5 milliseconds younger" (weak source).
- Horvath's DNA-methylation clock (Genome Biology 2013) was sourced but is not plain enough to say; not carried.

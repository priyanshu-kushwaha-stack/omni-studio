the first hotel of pods opened there in 1979 (c01), so rows of people lying side by side in two-metre boxes (c02) are
already the picture of people plugged in, and the only new thing in the frame is a cable. The hotel is also the engine:
your boss owns the pods, so every wild use of the power (the exam student, the surgeon, the judge, the bride, the morning
train) walks past your desk. A sleeper train (no same door every morning) and a hospital ward (illness colours the idea,
and beds in rows is tonight's other film) were weighed and dropped. It is deliberately unlike film 1 tonight (AI rents
your brain while you sleep): no bedroom, no blanket, no headband, no broker; you sit up out of a pod in a dark hall.
Roles, not names: your flatmate (named once, "your flatmate, Mio"), your boss, the old sleeper. The gag is "Tell me
tomorrow" against her brother's story, which she never gets to finish, and it ends "Tell me now". The birthday ("It was
yesterday.") is the cost at 60%; the slow room is the twist and the wordless peak.

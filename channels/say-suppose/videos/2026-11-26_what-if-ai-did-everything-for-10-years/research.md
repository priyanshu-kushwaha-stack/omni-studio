# Research: What If AI Did Everything for 10 Years?

The owner's handover (`handover/HANDOVER.md`, section 3) named seven papers. Each was checked on 2026-11-26 against
its own page or, where that refused the fetch, a column by the same authors. `claims.md` is the ledger the script
cites; this file adds what was not checked and what to re-read at G2.

The story is invented and carries no row: 2041, Echo, the lenses, the drones, the printed building, the city, your
job, your company, the vote, the solar storm, the water plant, every person and every number inside the story.

**Corrections made in research** (never say the left side): "about 65 percent" without AI becomes **67** (the Anthropic
article's text; its chart reads about 65) (c15); "almost twenty-seven thousand school students" are **secondary school
students, grades 7 to 12, in one county in central China** (c03); "finished 30 percent faster" is **45 minutes instead
of 64** (the column gives minutes, not a percent) (c05); "exam scores fell 20 percent" are **monthly closed-book exams,
against their own average before AI** (c06); the MIT brain study gives **no percentage** for quoting (c14); Zhifeng
Cai is at **Rutgers**, not MIT (c12); "two researchers at MIT" holds for Caosun and Aral (MIT Sloan) (c01).

## Verdicts

- **The Augmentation Trap** (Caosun and Aral, MIT Sloan, 2026): real; a model. Supports Year 1 and, with c02, Year 6.
- **The Generative AI Learning Penalty** (Strömberg, Lei and Wu, 2026): real; measured data from China, a working
  paper. The strongest fact in the film: homework up, exams down. It is the guess-then-flip the owner asked for.
- **The AI Layoff Trap** (Hemenway Falk and Tsoukalas, 2026): real; a model; already checked for the shelved film.
- **The AI Skill Trap** (Cai, Rutgers, 2026): real; a model with exactly the threshold the handover describes.
- **Gradual Disempowerment** (Kulveit and others, 2025): real; an argument, as the handover says.
- **Your Brain on ChatGPT** (Kosmyna and others, MIT Media Lab, 2025): real; 54 people, preprint, no percentage on
  quoting. The weakest card; the evaluation suggested folding it into one line or dropping it.
- **How AI Impacts Skill Formation** (Shen and Tamkin, Anthropic, 2026): real; 52 engineers; 50% against 67%. Its best
  detail for the ending: the AI users who asked conceptual questions scored 65% or more, those who delegated under 40%.

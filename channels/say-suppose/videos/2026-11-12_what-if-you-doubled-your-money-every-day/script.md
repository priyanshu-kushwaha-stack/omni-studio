> SITUATION: Berlin, a flat above Lotte's bakery. You know your balance to the cent, and every morning Jonas pays for your roll.
> DESIRE: To pay for your own bread, once; then to see how far one cent can go.
> CONFLICT: The money arrives faster than anything can be made to spend it on, and every doubling thins what everyone else holds, starting with Lotte's bakery.
> CHANGE: You stop trying to spend the number and start racing to make it exactly zero.
> RESULT: Zero at midnight, with two seconds to spare. Then Jonas's phone rips.
> UNDER: If all the money were mine, who would still bake the bread?
> DARLING: "You always know the number."
> DARLING: "It isn't taken from anyone. Except it is. From everyone. A little. Every night."
> DARLING: "Zero, doubled, is zero."

# Research: doubled your money every day (G1 restart)

Read on 2026-11-12, in this session. The spoken claims, their verbatim quotes and the sums are in `claims.md`; this
file is the reading behind them and why the film is set where it is.

## Why Berlin, a bakery, one cent

The film is about new money chasing the same goods. Its best real record is Germany's 1923 hyperinflation, and its
best price tag is a loaf of bread: a loaf in Berlin that cost around 160 marks at the end of 1922 cost 200 billion marks
by late 1923 (Wikipedia, "Hyperinflation in the Weimar Republic", c01). That period also gives the film its pictures:
banknotes used as wallpaper (the same article's photo caption, c03) and workers paid twice a day who spent their wages
at once (City AM on the Rentenmark, c02; Wikipedia "Zero stroke", citing Ahamed's *Lords of Finance*: workers paid at
the start of the day and given half an hour to buy goods). Lotte's framed note is a real 1923 denomination: the
Papiermark list has fifty-million-mark Reichsbanknotes dated 25 July and 1 September 1923 (c10).

Lotte's own night is the 1990 currency union (DDR Museum, "Western money at last!", c04): wages, salaries, pensions and
rents converted at 1:1; savings at 1:1 up to 2,000 (children to 14), 4,000 (adults to 59) or 6,000 GDR marks (60 and
over), and every higher sum at 2:1. The Bundesbank's March 2002 Monthly Report gives the same per-capita limits. Her
"half above the line" is that 2:1.

One cent, not one dollar: a cent is the smallest thing you can hold, so day thirty-one (2^30 cents, about €10.7 million,
"a billion cents") is still a joke, and the world arrives on day fifty-five (2^54 cents, about €180 trillion).

## The doubling and the chessboard

- The legend's total, 2^64 − 1 = 18,446,744,073,709,551,615 grains (Wikipedia, "Wheat and chessboard problem", c05).
  The same article: the grains weigh about 1,199,000,000,000 metric tons, "over 1,400 times the global production of
  wheat" (729 million tonnes in 2014; 842 million in 2025/26), so "more wheat than the whole world grows in over a
  thousand years" (c11). The film's chessboard is wheat, not rice.
- People asked to extend a doubling series grossly underestimate it (Wagenaar and Sagaria 1975, *Perception &
  Psychophysics* 18(6), 416-422, abstract; c08).
- Each doubling is larger than all the earlier ones added together: 2^n > 2^0 + ... + 2^(n-1) = 2^n − 1. Arithmetic, no
  row.

## Scale and the bank

- The world's yearly output: the IMF's 2026 projection of world nominal GDP, 128,003,776 million US$ (Wikipedia, "List of
  countries by GDP (nominal)", IMF column; c09). Day fifty-five, about €180 trillion, passes it in either currency.
- A bank can erase a number: in April 2024 Citigroup credited $81 trillion to a customer account instead of $280; a
  third employee caught it an hour and a half after it was processed and the entry was reversed several hours later
  (CBS News, reporting the FT of 28 February 2025; Bloomberg, Irish Times, CNN the same day; c07).
- Giving makes it worse: Mansa Musa's gifts in Cairo in 1324 depreciated gold there, which did not recover for at least
  twelve years (Wikipedia, "Mansa Musa", from al-Umari; c06).

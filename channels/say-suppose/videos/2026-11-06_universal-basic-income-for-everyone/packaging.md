# Packaging: universal basic income for everyone

status: awaiting the owner (G1)
topic, in the owner's words (2026-11-06): "What would happen if everyone if given Basic Universal Income ?"

## Premise

One morning the same payment lands in every adult's account, with no conditions, and it will land again every
month. You follow what you do with it, then what your street does, then what a whole country does.
The story is invented. What happens next is built from places that have tried a version of it: the script's
research starts with the trials in Finland, Kenya, Alaska, Iran, Canada and India, each read at the source.

## Thumbnail brief (A)

- Expression: eyes wide, mouth a small o, looking at the phone. Disbelief.
- Framing: you large on the left; a coin the size of your head beside the phone; four small figures behind,
  each with a phone and a coin.
- Words: NO STRINGS, on a paper tag, top right.
- Contrast: sky blue and pastel buildings behind; the mustard coin and head carry the frame at feed size.

# Research: universal basic income for everyone

Fetched and read on 2026-11-06. Every spoken claim, its exact quote and its source are in `claims.md`; each quote
was checked against the saved page text. This file is the reading behind them.

## What the evidence says

- **Work.** Most trials find people keep working. Alaska's yearly dividend (since 1982) had no effect on
  employment and raised part-time work (Jones & Marinescu, NBER 24312). Kenya's 12-year village UBI left work
  unchanged overall but moved people from wage jobs to their own businesses (Banerjee et al. 2023). Iran's
  universal transfers did not reduce work except among young people (Salehi-Isfahani & Mostafavi-Dehzooei, ERF
  1090). Finland saw no difference in year one and six more days in year two, confounded by a new sanction rule.
  **The main counter-evidence** is the OpenResearch trial in Texas and Illinois ($1,000 a month for three years,
  low-income people): participation fell 4.2 points and income from work by about $1,900 a year, with the time
  going mostly to leisure (Vivalt et al., NBER 32719).
- **Prices.** Local evidence says cash barely moves prices: 0.1% on average in Kenya's general-equilibrium study
  (Egger et al., NBER 26600). Iran's inflation rose sharply at the same time as large energy price rises; the
  authors put it down mainly to energy, while many Iranians blamed the transfers. No study measures a full
  national UBI.
- **Wellbeing and children.** Finland's recipients reported better wellbeing (no baseline survey). The Madhya
  Pradesh pilot (SEWA Bharat, UNICEF) reported more girls in secondary school and more paid work by women; it is
  small and was evaluated by the people who ran it. Canada's Mincome hospital finding (8.5% fewer) is disputed by
  a 2021 reanalysis (Green, UBC).
- **Cost.** India's Economic Survey 2016-17 illustrated a UBI of INR 7,620 a year costing 4.9% of GDP at 75%
  coverage. The OECD (2017) found a budget-neutral basic income far too small to end poverty and one at the
  poverty line very expensive.

## Story arithmetic (no row needed, spoken in words)

All of it is built on the invented counter, so none of it is a claim about the real world.

- Forty-two at the door: two trips home a year, the plan you have. The counter does the rest; the script never says
  what "the rest" is, and never counts down her life (G1 fact-checker).
- Thirty-eight visits, a weekend each: seventy-six days.
- Twenty-two years at home is about 8,000 days (22 × 365 = 8,030). Seventy-six more against eight thousand gone:
  about 99% already used, said as "closer to ninety-nine". It is the story's number for you, not Urban's (c01).

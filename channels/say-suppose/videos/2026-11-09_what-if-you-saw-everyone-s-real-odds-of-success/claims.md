Every real-world claim the narrator speaks. The source is the Press Information Bureau's release on the final result of
UPSC's Civil Services Examination 2025 (6 March 2026), read on 2026-11-09 in Chrome because PIB refuses scripted
fetches. Its text is saved in `sources/2026-11-09_pib-cse-2025-result-highlights.txt`; each quote below was checked
character for character against that file. One dataset, one exam, one year (fact-checker, G1 round 1).

teacher's three, the topper's eight, the chaiwala's sixty-one, Aarav's zero point two), the "one chair out of two
thousand five hundred" (your invented chip said in people), the stall, the mock test and the cousin. Where a spoken
figure is worked out from the source, the note gives the sum.

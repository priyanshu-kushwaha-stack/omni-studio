# Research: everyone's real odds of success

Read on 2026-11-09. The spoken claims, their quotes and sums are in `claims.md`; the source text is saved in
`sources/`. This file is the reading behind them and why this exam.

## Which exam

The film needs one entrance exam with seats against applicants from one official dataset (fact-checker, G1 round 1),
one that Riya (`audience.md`: 22, Pune, final-year exam season, chai outside the coaching centre) would plausibly sit
a coaching batch for. UPSC's Civil Services Examination fits: it is the exam a final-year student joins a night
batch for, Pune is a coaching city for it, and UPSC publishes every cycle's funnel in one release.

## What the source says (PIB, CSE 2025 result highlights, 6 March 2026)

| Stage | Candidates | Of those who applied |
|---|---|---|
| Applied | 9,37,876 | 100% |
| Appeared at the Preliminary | 5,76,793 | 61.5% |
| Qualified for the written Main | 14,161 | 1.5% |
| Qualified for the Personality Test (interview) | 2,736 | 0.29% |
| Recommended for appointment | 958 | 0.102% (1 in 979) |

v1 used three figures, one at a time; after G2 room round 1 only the first is spoken (story #7), and c02 and c03
stay in the ledger as dropped:

1. **The crowd's number** (c01): about one in a thousand applicants got in. This is the base rate every chip in
   the hall starts from.
2. **The belief breaker** (c02): nearly four in ten applicants never sat the paper. A crowd's number counts people
   who never showed up, so it is not a verdict on you; it moves with what you do.
3. **The late mind-boggle** (c03): of those who reached the interview room, about one in three got in. Same exam,
   same year, a very different number for the people that far down the road. Said without claiming that effort
   alone got them there.

## Not used

- PIB's release for CSE 2024 (PRID 2123422) shows the same shape in search results (9,92,599 applied, 5,83,213
  appeared, 1,009 recommended). PIB refused WebFetch (HTTP 403), and it was not read in the browser, so it is not
  cited.
- Coaching sites (Testbook, PW) give about 13.4 lakh applicants for CSE 2024, which contradicts the official
  9,92,599. Not used.
- No source for odds of a career in cricket, for chai stalls or for coaching fees: the cousin's and the chaiwala's
  chips are invented story numbers, said in words.

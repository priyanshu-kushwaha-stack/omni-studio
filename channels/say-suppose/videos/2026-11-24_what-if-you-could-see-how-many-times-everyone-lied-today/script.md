## Changed from the packaging, and why

1. **The spread is planted at the stadium.** New lines at about 4:40: "Two rows down. Someone counting the box on their fingers. The way you do. Can this spread? You don't know. You don't know how you got it." Why: the room's blocker 1, so "Nine?" is not a new rule after halfway.
2. **The crimson stranger appears three times.** At the stadium two rows down, the space over their head hidden by the railing; in the debate's front row with no counter at all; and at the peak counting on their fingers in step with you, then gone. Why: the room's blocker 2, no new person after 60%, and the stranger now matters at the peak.
3. **The 4:00 card reads only "54% (c06)".** The 47% and 61% are not spoken either; "a coin flip that wants to believe you" carries the lean. Why: the room's blocker 3.
4. **Poker night is causal.** A guest films the table; the clip is everywhere by Sunday, and that is why the morning show calls on Monday, and why the city knows about you by Tuesday. Why: the room's suggestion; the party's host became "someone" so the word "host" names only the television host.
5. **The habit change is shown at the close.** You type "Fine" to the boss, erase it letter by letter and send "Not really"; the narrator: "Typed lies don't count. You erase it anyway." Why: the room's suggestion, and it makes the counter's blind spot the last thing you act on.

> SITUATION: Your flat, your bus, your office floor and your city, where you say "fine", "nearly there" and "love it" a dozen times a day and have never once called any of them a lie.
> DESIRE: To never be fooled again, by anyone.
> CONFLICT: You can see the number over every head, and once the city knows it, everyone around you stops talking, the powerful stop speaking out loud, and the one number you cannot see is the one the whole country starts asking you for.
> CHANGE: You stop reading the numbers for your own sport and make the biggest liar in the country say his lies out loud, on air, line by line.
> RESULT: The country watches a number climb that only you can see, believes you anyway, and the next morning somebody reads the number over your own head back to you.
> UNDER: If you could see every lie but your own, would you still want to look?
> DARLING: "The city isn't lying to you. The city is mostly fine. The few who are have a corner office."
> DARLING: "You don't need to see your own. You know what you say."
> DARLING: "Host: Read it out loud. For the people who can't see."

# Script: What If You Fired Everyone and Hired AI?

> SITUATION: Your diner across the road from the car plant, forty stools you count every lunch as the whistle fills them, and a cook who is sick every Monday.
> DESIRE: To never again depend on a person showing up.
> CONFLICT: Every machine shift saves its owner a whole wage and costs the town a whole wage of spending, but only a slice lands on the owner who did it, so nobody stops, you least of all.
> CHANGE: You stop renting machine shifts cheap and charge each one the tab its worker would have run up at everyone else's counter.
> RESULT: The whistle fills the stools again, you still collect on every machine that stays, and the plant boss is eating on stool four.
> UNDER: If every owner in town is right to do it, how can the whole town be wrong?
> DARLING: "You didn't fire a cook. You fired a customer who happened to know how to cook."
> DARLING: "Everyone in town has done the sum. Everyone got it right. That's the problem."
> DARLING: "A machine never calls in sick. It never eats the pie, either."

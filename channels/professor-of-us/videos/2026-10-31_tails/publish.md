## Description (paste as is)
You had a tail, around week five in the womb, about a sixth of your body. By week eight it's gone. Apes lost theirs about 25 million years ago, and in 2024 scientists found a "jumping gene" in a tail gene that may have helped. The researchers suspect it came with a price.
